# Test1 — map brief (owner 2026-08-30)

One page, one-page-brief pattern (register row 35). Departures from this brief are listed
at the bottom as they happen.

## Atmosphere pin

A sun-bleached military training ground at mid-morning: dusty khaki earth, plywood
target silhouettes, sandbag lines and rust-streaked shipping containers under a hard,
clear sky. Everything looks government-issue, weathered, and slightly improvised.

## Inspiration boundary

Layout beats and mood are inspired by the classic firing-range training-map archetype
(Black Ops 1 era). ALL art is original and procedural — no ripped geometry, textures,
names, or logos; the in-game name is exactly "Test1".

## Layout (small COD-like sub-section, ~52 × 38 m, 180° rotational symmetry)

- **Centre: the tower.** A two-storey cinderblock range-control tower with an open
  observation deck — the map's power position, reachable by exterior stair. Sightlines
  from the deck cover both lanes but the climber is exposed.
- **West lane: the firing line.** Long open shooting lanes — sandbag walls at hip
  height, plywood silhouette targets on posts, a wooden awning shack at each end.
  The long-sightline duel lane.
- **East lane: the container yard.** Six shipping containers (two stacked pairs) making
  a tight CQB weave with one climbable low container onto a stack.
- **Ends: spawn sheds.** Each team spawns behind a plywood hut + berm cluster with two
  covered exits (one per lane).

## Gameplay contract

- Modes: TDM and FFA (existing modes; no new mode for Test1).
- Scale: tight — 6-player intended. Traversal: existing jump/autostep rules only.
- Cover census: every cover piece breaks both stance eye-lines or is a jump-mountable
  platform; no decorative near-cover.

## Procedural-art clause

Every texture and surface generated in code (canvas/PBR pipeline already in-repo):
dust/hardpan ground with tyre ruts, corrugated container steel, plywood, sandbag weave.
Props are presentation-only unless registered as cover. Audio: existing weapon/ambience
stack; no new audio work.

## Forbidden

No vehicles, no interior rooms beyond the tower's stair landing, no water, no
ladders/mantles (engine has none), no named-brand references, no new game modes.
