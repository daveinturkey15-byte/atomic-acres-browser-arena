# FARCRYSIS rework - the frozen brief

**Status: FROZEN.** Copied verbatim from
`docs/research/2026-09-04/FARCRYSIS-rework-plan.md` section 3.1 as Phase 0 of the rework
lane. R1 method step 1 is explicit that the brief is frozen as a document *before any code*,
including the negative space. **Do not edit this file mid-flight.** A change of direction is
a new dated brief beside this one, with the owner's word attached, not an edit to this one.

Arena id `farcrysis`. **Parked** (`selectable: false`) since HF-429 and it stays parked

## Subject, one sentence

A small equatorial island research station, flooded and abandoned long enough for the jungle
to have taken the concrete back: a lagoon-side beach ring, a dense mid-island jungle band,
and a broken reinforced-concrete core.

## Time and weather, one sentence

07:40, an hour after sunrise, clear with high haze after overnight rain - the sun is low and
warm from the east, every horizontal surface is still damp, and the air has enough moisture
to make one soft shaft through the canopy.

*(This one sentence fixes the entire light rig; it is not decoration.)*

## Photographic register

Documentary tropical-coast photography and dive/expedition stills: hard sun, deep but *open*
shade, colour in the shadows from bounced sand and foliage, no orange grade. Not a
film-look, not golden hour, not neon.

## Acceptance question - inverted for a shooter (R1 method step 16)

> *"Would a paused frame read as a photograph, AND can I read an enemy silhouette at 20 m
> standing in the deepest shade on the map?"*

Both, or it fails. A 5 EV sun-to-shade ratio and shaded walls at sRGB 46 are correct for a
photograph and **wrong for an arena**.

## DO NOT list

- No golden hour. No orange grade.
- No fog bank. One haze shaft, physically motivated, at most 0.004 of sun radiance per lit
  metre (R1 section 8).
- No lens flare. No bloom beyond the shipped chain's `strength ~= 0.045`. No neon.
- No imported assets of any kind. `authoring: 'code'` and
  `authoringNote: 'ALL CODE BUILD, NO ASSET IMPORT'` are a registry contract.
- No pure black and no pure white in any generator: blacks around 26 sRGB, whites around 220.
- No flat single-colour surface anywhere a critic camera can see (HF-451).
- No new clutter in the middle of the map.

## Originality boundary

Nothing in this rework copies geometry, texture data, shader code or trade dress from any
external game. A frame grab from Far Cry or Crysis is a **REJECTED** reference: it may not
be measured and it may never be put in front of a critic as "make it look like this." The
arena is an *original* homage; its bar is photography of the real world.

## Machine budget

Headless only, one browser at a time, ports 4280-4289, at least 3000 MiB free VRAM and an
empty ComfyUI queue before any capture, never on the owner's main screen. If VRAM does not
free within 20 minutes of polling, browser rows are marked **OPEN** - not skipped, not
assumed.

## What is preserved, and why this is not a rollback

The presentation layer is rebuilt completely. The **authority layer is preserved
byte-for-byte** unless a measurement says it is wrong:

- `farcrysis-terrain-authority.ts` and its test - the terrain collision proxy that took the
  HF-402 spawn-floor rule from 6.44 % to 100 % coverage.
- `farcrysis-constants.ts` - the solved spawn table, patrol anchors, `FARCRYSIS_BOUNDS`,
  `FARCRYSIS_MAX_SIGHTLINE = 22`, `FARCRYSIS_COVER_MIN = 14`.
- `farcrysis-physics.ts` - interactables, fuel drums, crate-lid / tower-deck / dish /
  cave-crown authority.
- The `FARCRYSIS-LOAD` region in `legacy-main.ts` and the cold-session precompile authority.
- **All 25 farcrysis test files.** A test may be extended; none may be weakened, and none may
  be deleted because its subject was rewritten.
- The admission-evidence guard and its red test in the publish script.
