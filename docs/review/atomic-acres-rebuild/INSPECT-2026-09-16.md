## Why two days produced little — the pipeline, not the effort

Full write-up: `docs/REBUILD_LANE_PIPELINE_CORRECTION_2026-09-16.md` on the branch.

1. **The reference loop was never closed.** 18 judge refs existed; nothing ever put a
   render beside one. Lanes were briefed in prose and graded against their own briefs, so
   a 580-triangle crate got nine 1024px maps (11.19 MB) while the ceiling stayed orange.
2. **A scene-graph census was accepted as visual evidence.** "21/21 crates resolved"
   proves the graph, not the frame.
3. **Claims outran reality.** The day-set was logged "integrated … (pushed)" while the
   load-bearing arena registration — 1,074 lines — sat in **no commit on any branch**.
   One `git checkout .` would have destroyed it. Rescued at 09:00.
4. **Lanes were budgeted on triangles** while texture residency ran unmeasured at 1.6 GB.

4. **`spawn-layout-quality` — 2 failures**, both world-studio's own no-cover-in-reach rows.

5. **Horizon reads as a ring from the overview camera.** Camera-locked because this arena's

## Decisions I made rather than interrupting you

- **Pushed the branch.** It was already on origin; the work is now durable off-machine.
- **Kept the `DEFAULT_ARENA_ID` flip** (`world-studio` → `atomic-acres-rebuild`), so the
  arena is the menu default. Isolated to one line and revertable alone.
- **Texture budget: 512**, with 1024 held for `interiors`, `ground`, and the five furniture
  atlases (those are 2048-sourced single atlases on props you walk up to; 512 measured
  PSNR 30.63 dB against 33.35 at 1024, for 21 MB of the 1212 saved).
- **meshopt deliberately NOT applied** — the rebuild loader has no Meshopt decoder and its
  catch swallows load failure into gray placeholder massing, so it would have silently
  turned every prop gray.
